Official institutional identity assets — editorial identification only.

The five original files in this directory are copied unchanged from actual
official website assets. They are not generated logos, extracted favicons,
recoloured icons, partnership badges or claims of institutional endorsement.
Original colours, aspect ratios and bytes are preserved. The full provenance
and exact guide mapping are in site/src/data/place-identities.js.

Changi Airport Group and Airports of Thailand identify airport operators.
Guy's and St Thomas' NHS Foundation Trust identifies the hospital's parent trust.
These scopes must be visibly labelled; they are not airport/hospital-specific
logos. The University of Toronto mark is only mapped to its own university guide;
its original white inverse variant uses a navy plate without recolouring.
Heathrow's mark specifically identifies its official Media Centre; that qualifier
is retained in the research record. It is not displayed as the airport identity.
Only the other four marks are eligible for institutional display.

Rights remain with the respective organisations. Public accessibility is not an
open copyright licence. Use is limited here to source-attributed editorial
identification, as requested. No claim of broad reuse permission is made.

The research found explicit restrictions for Warwick, UHN, Houston Methodist
and Aena. Those marks are not included or displayed. Schiphol, Melbourne and
Pearson have no imported usable mark in this pass (the manifest records the
specific access/source limitations). For these seven and Heathrow, the component uses neutral
text only. Detailed reasons and
restriction source links are retained in IDENTITY_COVERAGE in the manifest.
