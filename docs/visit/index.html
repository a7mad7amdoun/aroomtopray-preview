London Heathrow

Check your terminal and security side

  • Heathrow links its prayer rooms to terminal maps.
  • Wudu facilities are listed for specific rooms; check your room before you go.
  • Religious items in the room cupboards may be borrowed.

Return shared items after use.

Manchester Airport

Before security in T3

  • Terminal 3 has no prayer room after security.
  • Its arrivals prayer room is reached by stairs or lift.

Check your terminal and security side.
