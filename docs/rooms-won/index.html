Location, access and facilities, in one place. Show room

In 13 cases, the source says people asked for the room. Where a source doesn’t say so, we don’t either.

  1. 2026
    June 2026 Airport People asked

    Seattle-Tacoma International Airport (SEA)

    SeaTac, Washington, United States

    A second interfaith prayer and meditation room opened in Concourse C, with a foot-washing area and a floor inlay marking the qibla.

    The source credits years of work by a multi-faith group that included the Muslim Association of Puget Sound.

    Source: Seattle Airport Opens Second Prayer and Meditation Room (FāVS News)

  2. February 2026 Airport

    Leeds Bradford Airport (LBA)

    Leeds, United Kingdom

    A new landside multi-faith room opened with prayer mats, a qibla compass and shoe storage; the airport says an airside room with ablution facilities is planned for early 2027.

    Delivered with a local business partner, according to the airport.

    Source: Leeds Bradford Airport supports inclusive travel with opening of new multi-faith room (Leeds Bradford Airport)

  3. 2025
    August 2025 (announced) University People asked

    Harvard University

    Cambridge, Massachusetts, United States

    The university designated a permanent Muslim prayer room in the Smith Campus Center from fall 2025, replacing temporary spaces, with a foot-washing station being installed.

    The source says the move followed student requests and a university task force recommendation for permanent Muslim prayer spaces.

    Source: Harvard University Opens Permanent Muslim Prayer Space, Strengthening Religious Inclusion on Campus (Rakwa)

  4. 2024
    September 2024 Airport People asked

    LaGuardia Airport (LGA), Terminal C

    New York, United States

    An interfaith chapel built by Delta Air Lines was dedicated in Terminal C; a Muslim chaplain led prayer there after the ceremony.

    The source describes Catholic clergy pressing for a chapel, including raising it with Delta's chief executive.

    Source: LaGuardia Finally Opens Interfaith Chapel for Travelers (The Tablet)

  5. August 2024 Airport

    East Midlands Airport (EMA)

    Castle Donington, United Kingdom

    The airport's first multi-faith room after security opened in the departure lounge, with wash facilities and a divider so two groups can use it at once.

    Run by the airport chaplaincy team.

    Source: New multi faith room opens at EMA (East Midlands Airport)

  6. July 2024 University People asked

    Medical University of South Carolina (MUSC)

    Charleston, South Carolina, United States

    Two multifaith prayer and meditation rooms opened on the Charleston campus for students, staff and faculty, so Muslim students no longer had to rely on the hospital chapel.

    The source says the rooms followed a multiyear effort led by the MUSC Muslim Student Association.

    Source: MUSC establishes multifaith prayer and meditation room for staff, students and faculty (MUSC Center for Global Health)

  7. June 2024 Airport People asked

    Los Angeles International Airport (LAX), Tom Bradley International Terminal

    Los Angeles, United States

    A multi-faith quiet room for prayer, reflection and meditation opened near gate 130.

    CAIR-LA thanks a community activist for coordinating with LAX over six years.

    Source: CAIR-LA Welcomes Opening of Multi-Faith Prayer Room at LAX (CAIR)

  8. March 2024 Hospital People asked

    Fieldhead Hospital (South West Yorkshire Partnership NHS Foundation Trust)

    Wakefield, United Kingdom

    The trust opened its first purpose-built wuḍūʾ facility, opposite the prayer room, ahead of Ramadan.

    The trust says it acted on feedback and requests from colleagues, patients and visitors.

    Source: Supporting our Muslim colleagues… Trust launches wudhu facility at Fieldhead Hospital (South West Yorkshire Partnership NHS Foundation Trust)

  9. February 2024 Airport

    George Bush Intercontinental Airport (IAH), Terminal D

    Houston, Texas, United States

    Houston Airports opened its first ablution room and a connected prayer room, which can be divided by a partition, near gate D15.

    The release says airport chaplains were working with Houston mosques on the qibla.

    Source: Houston Airports refines international passenger experience with new Ritual Washing and Prayer spaces at Bush Airport (Houston Airports)

  10. 2023
    October 2023 Airport

    Newcastle International Airport (NCL)

    Newcastle upon Tyne, United Kingdom

    A refurbished multi-faith prayer room and quiet space near security reopened, open 24 hours with prayer mats and religious texts.

    Faith leaders from the region, including an imam, attended the opening.

    Source: Brand new multi-faith prayer room opens at Newcastle International Airport (Newcastle International Airport)

  11. January 2023 Hospital People asked

    The Queen Elizabeth Hospital

    King's Lynn, United Kingdom

    New multi-faith rooms opened with separate prayer rooms and ablution rooms for men and women, open 24 hours to patients, families and staff.

    The trust says it opened the rooms in direct response to patient and staff needs and feedback.

    Source: QEH opens new multi-faith rooms (The Queen Elizabeth Hospital King's Lynn NHS Foundation Trust)

  12. 2022
    March 2022 University People asked

    University of Maryland

    College Park, Maryland, United States

    An ablution room with private washing stations opened in the Stamp Student Union.

    The Muslim Student Association president says it was one of the priorities students pushed for.

    Source: After three years of planning, UMD opens ablution room for Muslim community (The Diamondback)

  13. 2021
    April 2021 Airport

    Seattle-Tacoma International Airport (SEA)

    SeaTac, Washington, United States

    An interfaith prayer and meditation room opened after security, below Concourse A.

    Buddhist, Muslim, Christian and Jewish leaders advised on the design, according to the Port.

    Source: Sensory Room and Interfaith Prayer and Meditation Room Open at SEA Airport (Port of Seattle)

  14. 2019
    December 2019 Hospital

    Harrogate District Hospital

    Harrogate, United Kingdom

    A charitable donation paid for new ablution facilities for Muslim staff, patients and visitors in the chaplaincy centre.

    Source: Charitable donation goes towards create new Muslim prayer ablution facilities (Harrogate and District NHS Foundation Trust)

  15. November 2019 Hospital

    Cleveland Clinic Abu Dhabi

    Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates

    A multi-faith prayer room opened, described as the first at a healthcare facility in Abu Dhabi; the hospital already had Muslim prayer rooms for men and women.

    Source: Abu Dhabi hospital opens multi-faith prayer room (Gulf News)

  16. February 2019 University

    Mount Royal University

    Calgary, Alberta, Canada

    Two accessible ablution stations opened near the university's meditation room; they opened in late 2018 and were celebrated in February 2019.

    Students were consulted on the designs, according to the university.

    Source: Mount Royal University: New ablution stations open to Muslim students and employees (Education News Canada)

  17. 2018
    November 2018 Airport

    Abu Dhabi International Airport (AUH), Terminal 3

    Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates

    A multi-faith prayer room opened next to the bus gates in Terminal 3.

    Source: Abu Dhabi International Airport opens multi-faith prayer room (Passenger Terminal Today)

  18. 2017
    December 2017 University People asked

    Arizona State University

    Tempe, Arizona, United States

    An interfaith reflection room and private ablution rooms were added to the Memorial Union.

    ASU's facilities chief says students' wishes drove the project, which students paid for.

    Source: ASU adds interfaith, ablution rooms to Memorial Union to accommodate religious practices (Cronkite News)

  19. November 2017 Airport People asked

    Seattle-Tacoma International Airport (SEA)

    SeaTac, Washington, United States

    The airport reopened its prayer and meditation room above the ticketing area, closed since 2015, and committed to building a room inside security.

    The source says the reopening followed collective pressure from Buddhist and Muslim leaders, with Jewish and Christian support.

    Source: Sea-Tac Airport re-opens Prayer, Meditation Room, Plans New One Past Security (Northwest Dharma Association)

  20. July 2017 Airport

    Christchurch Airport (CHC)

    Christchurch, New Zealand

    Prayer rooms for several faiths opened at the airport, with faith representatives at the ceremony.

    Source: Opening of Multi Religious Prayer Rooms (Fo Guang Shan New Zealand)

  21. 2016
    December 2016 Hospital People asked

    Netcare Christiaan Barnard Memorial Hospital

    Cape Town, South Africa

    Larger Muslim prayer rooms for men and women, with an ablution area, were built into the hospital's new building.

    A surgeon at the hospital argued for the expansion and helped the project team, according to Netcare.

    Source: Muslim prayer rooms at new Cape Town hospital (Netcare)

  22. March 2016 University

    University of Miami

    Coral Gables, Florida, United States

    An ablution area was added to the University Center space used for prayer, so students no longer had to use the public bathroom.

    Source: UM Adds Ablution Room for Muslims (University of Miami News)

  23. 2015
    November 2015 Airport

    London Luton Airport (LTN)

    Luton, United Kingdom

    A new airside multi-faith prayer room opened near gate 20, open around the clock, with washing facilities nearby.

    The airport chaplaincy advised on the design.

    Source: LLA opens new multi-faith Prayer Room (London Luton Airport)

  24. September 2015 Airport

    Orlando International Airport (MCO)

    Orlando, Florida, United States

    A reflection and prayer room opened on Airside 4, timed with the start of Emirates service from Dubai.

    Source: The Orlando Airport Has Opened a Muslim Prayer Room (Truth or Fiction)

  25. June 2015 Airport People asked

    George Best Belfast City Airport (BHD)

    Belfast, United Kingdom

    A multi-faith quiet room opened off the foyer, with scriptures from several faiths, including Islam.

    The airport's honorary chaplain persuaded management to add the room, according to the source.

    Source: Multi-Faith Quiet Room opens in George Best City Airport (Diocese of Down and Dromore)

  26. 2014
    2014 Airport

    Tokyo Haneda Airport (HND), Terminal 3

    Tokyo, Japan

    A prayer room opened in the international terminal; the operator says almost 2,000 people a month used it on average in fiscal 2024.

    Source: Where can we pray?: As Muslim tourism grows, Japan rethinks public spaces to meet needs (Malay Mail)

  27. 2013
    September 2013 University

    University of St. Thomas

    St. Paul, Minnesota, United States

    Renovated prayer rooms and foot-washing stations for Muslim students opened in Loras Hall.

    Source: Muslim Prayer Spaces Now Open for Use in Loras Hall (St. Thomas Newsroom)

  28. 2012
    October 2012 University People asked

    University of Oxford

    Oxford, United Kingdom

    A dedicated 24-hour Muslim prayer space with ablution areas for men and women opened in the Robert Hooke Building.

    The student union passed a motion for a dedicated Islamic prayer space in 2011, and the university had held talks with the Islamic Society.

    Source: Oxford University opens new dedicated Muslim prayer room (The Oxford Student)
