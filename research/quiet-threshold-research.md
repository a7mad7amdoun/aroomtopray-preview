## Useful additions

### Melbourne Airport: Ask a terminal ambassador for directions

Melbourne Airport directs unsure travellers to its Experience Ambassadors. The chaplaincy office is on Level 2 in Terminal 2; its phone is monitored around the clock.

[Official source](https://www.melbourneairport.com.au/find-support-from-melbourne-airports-chaplaincy)

Gap: The existing MEL record has two room locations and hours, but no human wayfinding fallback.

Limit: A continuously monitored phone does not mean the office is staffed continuously. This is Melbourne-specific advice.

### University of Bristol — Woodland Prayer Rooms: Arrange card access before an evening visit

For 24-hour access to the prayer rooms at 21 Woodland Road, Bristol asks users to email the Multifaith Chaplaincy to activate their Ucard.

[Official source](https://www.bristol.ac.uk/multifaith-chaplaincy/places-to-worship/)

Gap: Useful distinction between published opening hours and an access card that must first be enabled; not in current VisitInsights.

Limit: This page describes facilities for university students and staff. Do not imply public access or create a new directory record from this tip alone.

### UCL Great Ormond Street Institute of Child Health: Keep shared rooms available for prayer

GOS ICH’s G01 prayer room at 30 Guilford Street is not bookable. Its guidance asks users to use the shoe shelves, avoid phones and laptops inside, and take personal belongings away.

[Official source](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/population-health-sciences/child-health/about/equality-diversity-and-inclusion/quiet-rooms)

Gap: Different room and purpose from existing UCL Pharmacy insight; adds shared-space etiquette and no-booking distinction.

Limit: Applies specifically to these GOS ICH rooms, not every UCL room. The page welcomes staff, students and visitors but does not detail building-entry procedure.

### Ashford Hospital and St Peter’s Hospital, Surrey: A locked door may have a staffed access route

Both centres list daily opening from 08:00 to 18:00. The Trust says security staff can unlock them outside those hours when needed.

[Official source](https://ashfordstpeters.nhs.uk/our-multi-faith-centres)

Gap: Practical after-hours route beyond a bare closed/open label; absent from current hospital records and VisitInsights.

Limit: Do not label the rooms unconditionally open 24/7. Security assistance is an access process, not a guaranteed immediate entry time. Do not create new records from this tip alone.

### Zagreb Airport: Shared spaces cannot always be reserved

Zagreb’s airport prayer room is shared across faiths. Its official guidance says no group may reserve it or take exclusive use, and links to the airport map for its position.

[Official source](https://www.zag.aero/en/passengers/at-the-airport/facilities-services/prayer-room/116)

Gap: Clarifies shared-room expectations without inventing room-specific services; absent from current VisitInsights.

Limit: This rule is specific to Zagreb. The source does not state hours, security side, wudu provision or separate spaces; leave these unknown.
