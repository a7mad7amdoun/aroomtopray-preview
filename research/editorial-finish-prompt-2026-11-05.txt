A ROOM TO PRAY — QUIET HOSPITALITY, PRECISE WAYFINDING

ROLE
Work as a senior product designer and frontend engineer. Refine the approved
prayer-room experience into a calm, confident, beautifully finished public guide.
The visitor may be tired, anxious, carrying luggage, or helping someone else.
Luxury here means care, clarity, comfort and reliable information.

CREATIVE DIRECTION
A quiet private lounge within a busy public place. Let architecture communicate
peace, photography communicate location, and typography communicate certainty.
Keep the established room, stationary panoramic glass, rug facing the solid niche,
sacred calligraphy, and actual location photographs. Their successful geometry and
meaning are more valuable than another visual reset.

MATERIAL AND COLOR
Refine the light theme toward warm ivory and pale stone with midnight-blue ink.
Give dark mode deep navy backgrounds and slightly raised blue reading surfaces.
Use Hamza's yellow for destinations and selected states. Avoid metallic gradients,
fake gold textures, ornamental frames, or claims that this is a private service.
Each surface must have explicit, readable foreground colors. Retain status colors
and the airport's high-contrast departures-board palette.

TYPOGRAPHY
Keep Archivo as the coherent navigation and information voice, the existing
Garamond invitation, and the proper Arabic type system. Do not add more fonts.
Preserve the Adhan wording and Allah artwork. Use confident sizing and restrained
weights, generous paragraph leading, and readable metadata. Arabic has its own
line-height and no Latin tracking tricks. Preserve all four languages.

ARRIVAL AND PRIMARY ACTION
Keep the room as the hero. The search link beside the invitation should read as
an unmistakable action: a quiet navy button, light label, directional arrow, and
visible keyboard focus. Maintain direct native navigation to search. Give the
search surface and coverage figures a deliberate hierarchy, with more breathing
room on desktop and no fixed text heights. Preserve the recent category-intro
refinements, where the room and text have balanced proportions.

NAVIGATION
Keep the large brand, three primary destinations, search and language menu.
Use precise corners, a gentle separator/shadow and still, legible selected states.
The menu should feel like a carefully typeset visitor directory. Do not add a
second menu, duplicate preference controls, or new navigation gestures.

DESTINATION PHOTOGRAPHS
Present photographs like documentary prints with a modest inset and a consistent
frame. Keep their full content, original colors, provenance, and descriptive text.
On desktop give the three destination cards the same landscape presentation and
align information beneath. On mobile keep compact image-and-information rows.
Make focus and hover useful and calm, without translating, tilting, zooming or
hiding content. Do not confuse photo examples with proof of room access.

INFORMATION SYSTEM
Carry the same measured spacing, surface tones and restrained shadows through
reading panels, tabs, detail cards and galleries. Preserve each category's useful
character: airport sign, university directory, hospital information board.
Keep location, restrictions, facilities, sources and next actions readable.
Never invent or remove place information, access conditions, dates or affiliations.
Keep search, filters, maps, saved places and progressive/no-JS behavior intact.

IMPLEMENTATION
Use existing components and owning stylesheets. Adjust existing tokens instead
of adding a parallel palette. Keep Astro, existing data and architecture. Do not
include the unrelated pending map work. Make a focused presentation change with
no additional dependencies, image requests or runtime scripts.

ACCEPTANCE
Build all routes; run existing tests. Compare category/home content and links;
exercise localized directory behavior and validate export paths. Check contrast
for the revised surface/text combinations mathematically. Review responsive source
rules at360,390,760,1024 and1440px. State honestly that browser rendering remains
unavailable in this session; do not claim visual perfection or device testing.
Publish only Ahmed's authorized GitHub Pages preview, never Hamza's live website.
