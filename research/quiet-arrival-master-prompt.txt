A ROOM TO PRAY — QUIET ARRIVAL
Executable creative and product brief for a fresh presentation

PURPOSE

Build a new presentation of A Room to Pray around a single promise: finding somewhere to pray should feel calmer, clearer and easier. Translate Hamza’s practical directory into a beautifully considered arrival service. The visitor may be tired, short of time, unfamiliar with a campus, waiting for a relative, or approaching a flight connection. Give that person the care associated with an excellent first-class experience: immediate orientation, generous space, useful answers and nothing to decipher. This care is for everyone. It must never suggest a premium membership, exclusive access or a commercial hospitality service.

This is a new visual and interaction direction, not another variation of the previous simulated room. Preserve the real product beneath it: every existing place record, translation, source, filter, map capability, visitor tool and accessible route. Replace presentation complexity where necessary, without deleting valuable functionality. Work only on the authorized preview repository. Do not modify Hamza’s production website, DNS, databases or credentials. Preview submissions must remain disabled.

THE IDEA

Quiet Arrival combines two experiences into one coherent page: the emotional relief of reaching a peaceful threshold, and the practical confidence of knowing where to go. The architectural room is a signature editorial image, not the website’s operating system. Search, navigation and information must work immediately without opening a graphical door, exploring a scene or waiting for animation.

The English opening is “A place to pray. A moment of peace.” Follow it with a short, literal explanation of what the guide provides. Place the main search within the initial reading sequence. The visitor should understand the service, choose a setting and begin a search without scrolling through decorative content.

ART DIRECTION

Create one cinematic, believable architectural threshold. Think of quiet mineral walls, restrained timber, a clear opening, gentle daylight and a view of the busy world beyond. The architecture should feel possible to build. Keep perspective, shadows, material scale, joins and light direction consistent. Avoid theatrical arches, extravagant hotel staging, floating objects, religious clichés, impossible reflections and excessive furnishings. Calm must come from proportion and restraint.

Use a static high-quality illustration with photographic realism. Do not build another rotating CSS door or layer decorative handles, posters and controls over an image. The opening can suggest welcome without requiring a simulated opening interaction. Supply coordinated light and dark treatments with matching composition and readable contextual imagery. Dark mode should feel like a naturally lit evening room, not an underexposed image beneath a black overlay.

Give each category an appropriate setting through considered imagery: airport circulation, campus architecture or a hospital’s quiet interior context. The categories remain one visual family. Never imply that conceptual artwork depicts a named listed facility. Label it as conceptual illustration and distinguish it from any properly credited institutional photograph. Keep functional text, signage labels and controls in the interface, not baked into generated pixels.

IDENTITY AND COMPOSITION

Use Hamza’s ivory, deep navy or charcoal, and clear yellow as the identity foundation. Ivory provides warmth and reading space. Navy gives structure and confidence. Yellow marks a chosen state or useful action. Avoid gold gradients, neon effects, colorful category themes and decorative badge collections. Define semantic tokens for both themes so contrast is intentional rather than patched individually.

Use one unified typographic direction, with a compatible Arabic family where needed. Establish a small, disciplined scale for the brand, principal heading, section heading, body, labels and supporting metadata. Keep body copy comfortably sized and line lengths controlled. Do not let category pages introduce unrelated display fonts. Give Arabic equivalent prominence, balanced leading and correct RTL flow. Isolate airport codes, URLs and mixed-direction identifiers where needed.

Build a stable grid with consistent content widths, spacing and alignment. Let a large artwork area create a deliberate pause beside or after useful content. Do not place dense facts on imagery. Use few surfaces: a page background, a reading surface and a clearly selected state. Avoid stacks of frosted cards, tiny pills, floating decorative boxes and competing borders. On small screens, preserve the reading order before the artwork and crop intentionally around the threshold.

NAVIGATION AND SEARCH

Make the A Room to Pray wordmark visible and assured. Keep Airports, Universities and Hospitals as the three main destinations. Integrate language and appearance preferences into a clear secondary control area with accessible names and visible feedback. Do not duplicate these controls in the artwork.

Search must support the existing useful inputs and preserve its current capabilities. Give it a clear label, readable suggestions, understandable empty states and an obvious next action. Present results with the place name, city or country, and any essential access limitation. Maintain query, category and filter context across relevant transitions. Use progressive disclosure for secondary filters; do not hide active selections or the reset action.

Use country names alongside recognizable flags. Flags aid recognition but never carry the meaning alone. Keep one consistent icon family with text labels, including location, access and source status. Decorative icons must not resemble interactive controls.

DIRECTORIES AND PLACE PAGES

Treat each place page as an arrival card. Lead with the institution name, a concise location summary and the most important restriction. Follow with the six practical answers in consistent labeled rows or an equivalent scannable structure. Keep existing tabs but make their names and selected states unmistakable. Put supplementary narrative after the essential information.

Retain maps as useful companions to the result list. A selected marker and its corresponding result must visibly agree. Controls should have accessible labels and clear focus states. Do not invent routes, walking times, indoor positions or boundaries. Preserve the existing distinction between an institution’s location and a verified room location. Keep official map or institution links wherever already available.

Keep saved places, planners, preparation guidance and other working visitor tools discoverable in a consistent utility area. Offer the next relevant action once. Do not repeat a search field or a cluster of actions simply to fill space. When a room is not listed, preserve the useful path to asking the institution rather than implying that no room exists.

TRUTH AND ACCESSIBILITY

Retain original attribution and link each listing to Hamza’s corresponding page. Keep cited institutional sources and independently researched additions distinguishable. Do not invent opening hours, facilities, eligibility, institutional endorsement, verification dates, reviews, photos or availability. Unknown information remains visibly unknown. Never call a room “open now” from incomplete data.

Maintain native links and buttons, logical headings, skip navigation, keyboard operation, visible focus, sufficient contrast and useful form errors. Use motion only for brief state changes and respect reduced-motion preferences. The new visual concept must remain understandable without animation, imagery or precise pointer control.

EXECUTION AND ACCEPTANCE

Inspect the existing implementation before replacing presentation components. Reuse reliable data and behavior. Implement shared design tokens and components before making page-specific exceptions. Keep artwork assets optimized, properly sized and clearly credited. Avoid unnecessary dependencies and avoid downloading large media for modest placements.

Review home, all three directories, representative place pages and supporting tools in English, French, Arabic and Spanish; light and dark modes; mobile and desktop. Check search, filters, tabs, map selection, language switching, saved context, source links and existing visitor tools. Verify the production preview base path and disabled submissions. Inspect rendered pages when available; explicitly report any unavailable visual checks rather than claiming a visual review.

The finished result should feel unmistakably new while remaining unmistakably A Room to Pray. Judge every element by three questions: does it help the visitor understand, does it help the visitor act, or does it create a measured moment of calm? Remove elements that do none of these. Deliver a useful guide with an emotional identity, not an elaborate scene with a directory attached.
