A ROOM TO PRAY — THE FINISH OF A WELL-KEPT PUBLIC ROOM

Refine the approved website, not its direction. The continuous prayer room,
stationary glass, paired generated homepage views, real destination photographs,
conventional hospital scene, sacred calligraphy, prayer mat, typography and palette
are successful and must remain intact. Preserve all factual data and tools.

Use Pinterest references as design research, not as assets to copy:
- Schiphol's directional signs: one clear hierarchy, generous spacing, aligned
  symbols, strong dark/light contrast, yellow reserved for meaningful emphasis.
- University of Washington campus directories: coherent identifier, destination
  and direction columns; make labels useful without adding decorative clutter.
- Royal Children's Hospital wayfinding: readable grouping, reassuring ordinary
  symbols, enough white space around important information.
- The user's earlier oak-framed noticeboard and translucent plaque references:
  carry their quiet edges and orderly spacing into the existing surfaces, without
  copying pinned content or introducing fake institutional or clinical signage.

APPLY
1. Navigation: keep the brand and three primary destinations. Make the closed menu
   visually quieter, with a deliberate open state. Mark the current supporting
   page in the menu. Align its icons and labels without adding more controls.
2. Destination notices: establish one shared edge, an aligned arrow area and clear
   pointer/focus feedback. Keep the entire destination link clickable. Remove
   duplicated focus outlines; retain a strong accessible focus indicator.
3. Room-to-guide transition: preserve the return action and clarify its direction
   with an upward arrow. Keep room controls readable and separate from the artwork.
4. Reading surfaces: improve paired-fact alignment, retain essential restrictions
   prominently, and correct physical corner rules that do not mirror in Arabic.
   Unify small control radii and spacing using the existing local tokens.
5. Footer: finish the page with a clear boundary, a properly sized brand, a generous
   description column, readable navigation and consistent touch targets. On phones,
   let the introductory text span the width rather than squeeze into a narrow cell.
6. Galleries: preserve real photographs and complete captions. Improve the control
   alignment and frame spacing using the same signs-and-paper language. No autoplay,
   zoom effects, synthetic photographic tint, or new decorative icons.
