A ROOM TO PRAY — FROM SANCTUARY TO DESTINATION

DESIGN OBJECTIVE
Refine the airport, university and hospital directory entrances into a more spatial
experience. The visitor begins inside a prayer room. As they move into the guide,
the architecture quietly withdraws and the view through the glass becomes clearer
and closer. This is a transition in attention: from finding peace to understanding
where to go. The text remains the stable reference throughout.

Preserve the established service identity: Archivo information typography, warm
ivory, midnight navy, purposeful yellow, real place photography, the existing room,
stationary architectural glass, and truthful guide content. Keep the successful
masthead, destination gallery, reception area and responsive guide indexes from the
previous release. This is a focused enhancement of the room/category relationship,
not another unrelated style change.

LAYER THE ROOM PROPERLY
The current category room fades as one whole canvas. Split its rendering behavior
into independent architectural and destination layers. The room-wall artwork contains
the rug and niche, so fade that plate together rather than trying to extract or
repaint the rug. Retain the original wall image, exact mat placement, daylight and
nighttime geometry. Never turn or relocate the mat.

The glass/frame/exterior layer should remain much more visible than the architecture
and gently increase in size as the visitor scrolls. Anchor the enlargement to the
left side of the opening so it does not move toward or overlap the reading column.
Keep the maximum transformation modest enough that the frame stays inside the
architectural viewport and the picture remains believable. The window is a quiet
framing device, not an opening door or a teleportation tunnel.

Use one scroll progress measurement and one requestAnimationFrame batch. Apply a
smoothstep curve so neither end snaps. Scrolling upward must restore exactly the
same wall, rug and window without delayed interpolation or chasing the scroll.
Keep geometry changes out of the document flow. The scene can transform; the guide
must not change width, move vertically, fade or lose focus as a consequence.

ARRIVAL MOTION
A freshly loaded category may use a restrained, short approach toward the exterior
inside the glass. Clip that movement within the window. Keep glass reflections and
frame aligned. Do not scale text, the rug, navigation or controls. Do not hide the
room while waiting for artwork, and do not make navigation wait for animation.
A static fallback must be complete and useful.

As the visitor scrolls, let the room wall and rug fade more strongly than the glass.
The retained exterior is a context cue. It should not become a fixed, oversized
photo behind the entire results list. Let the whole architectural section eventually
leave the viewport normally. The guide below must retain its full reading width.

SACRED CALLIGRAPHY
The name of Allah belongs only to the homepage. Render the existing authentic SVG
there and nowhere else: not in category introductions, individual place pages,
visit pages or supporting pages. This is a source-template decision, not CSS that
leaves unwanted alt text or hidden content in other documents.

Preserve the SVG paths, Arabic wording, position above the niche and existing theme
color treatment. Do not regenerate, stylize, abbreviate or replace the calligraphy.
On supported same-origin browser navigation, let its outgoing snapshot fade gently
when leaving home. Name only this element for the transition. Disable root snapshots
so the whole page, room proportions and text are never stretched or crossfaded into
one another. Returning home may reveal the inscription softly.

Use native progressive enhancement; ordinary links must work without it. Do not
intercept clicks, insert timeouts, simulate another document, or restore removed
calligraphy on category pages. Under reduced motion, use immediate native navigation
and stable imagery. The transient outgoing snapshot is not persistent destination
content. It must not capture pointer input or cover controls for a long period.

TEXT AND SPACING
Keep a clear division between the left architectural view and the right introduction.
Use the existing grid and cap the reading card width so wide monitors do not produce
long lines. Give the heading a calmer weight, balanced wrapping and comfortable
leading. Keep the category emblem and labels proportional rather than decorative.
The paragraph should be easy to read quickly; the search area remains distinctly
separated below it. Preserve every translated word and every input label.

Maintain the airport's navy departures-sign surface and explicit light text. Give
university and hospital introductions their existing theme-aware surface with a
subtle warm wayfinding accent. Avoid a second font family for each category, a
metallic texture, bright neon, translucent text panels or low-contrast copy over
photography. Use existing semantic tokens and logical margins and borders.

On phones, keep the room in its own row and the information in a full-width reading
area. Preserve the compact header and existing stacking order so search remains easy
to reach. Limit the zoom further on narrow screens if the scene would otherwise
crowd the composition. Do not force a desktop split into a360px viewport. Arabic
reading direction must work without mirroring the real architectural room.

MOTION AND STABILITY CONTRACT
Scope the new split-room behavior to the three category directories. Homepage,
detail and supporting pages retain their existing decorative fade except for the
explicit calligraphy removal. Avoid broad changes to unrelated scripts.

Reduced motion must restore neutral scale and full architectural visibility. Invalid
or zero-size geometry must produce a safe, opaque room. Resize, content reflow,
mobile keyboard changes, browser Back/Forward and page visibility must use the
existing lifecycle handling. Hidden documents must not schedule new animation frames.
Repeated scroll events should collapse into one pending frame and repeated identical
values should not cause additional style writes.

Do not add a new dependency, router, stylesheet solely for overrides, autoplay loop,
scroll-jacking library or image-processing pipeline. Edit the owning component and
styles. Store explanatory comments where the layers and CSS properties connect.
Keep actual information, sources, saved places, tabs, maps, filters and languages
unchanged. Leave unrelated pending map work untouched.

VERIFICATION
Add meaningful behavioral tests for the directory layer values, bounded zoom,
scroll reversal, reduced-motion restoration and unchanged search/focus context.
Check source output across all page types to prove that the calligraphy exists
exactly on the four localized homepages and nowhere else. Compare guide content and
controls with the previous release. Run the existing suite and export checks.
Review source geometry at360,390,760,1024 and1440px. Inspect all edited CSS syntax.

Rendered browser checks remain unavailable under the existing session limitation.
Report that honestly. Native navigation-transition support is progressive, and its
appearance cannot be claimed as visually verified here. Do not equate unit tests
with a visual approval. Publish only Ahmed's GitHub Pages preview after the checks;
never modify Hamza's live website.
