Refinement prompt and implementation direction, 4 November 2026
Starting point: quiet-threshold preview, commit 3756cbbf3.

THE OBJECTIVE
Refine the current successful room experience into a clearer, more comfortable guide. Preserve Ahmed's left-hand doorway, Hamza's useful information and airport-sign identity, the six matched destination scenes, the warm prayer room, the gentle transition into reading, and the established typefaces. Make the existing artwork feel more considered through composition and restraint. This is a focused visual refinement, not a replacement design, framework migration, or new set of decorative features.

The visitor may be tired, anxious or in a hurry. The interface should communicate welcome without asking for attention it does not need. Make the next action evident. Keep a calm architectural setting around a readable, trustworthy directory. Premium means considerate proportions, useful hierarchy and careful finishing, not exclusivity, glossy surfaces or small type.

THE DESTINATION GALLERY
Keep the three real, credited destination photographs and the entire-card navigation. Give each destination a shared 3:2 image composition on narrow and wide cards. Let the photograph carry atmosphere, a compact navy-and-yellow sign carry identity, and the theme's ivory or navy reading surface carry description and coverage. Keep words outside the photograph so the scene remains uninterrupted and the text remains legible.

Separate the destination title from the supporting information by hierarchy, not multiple large dark bands. All three categories share the same typography, arrow treatment, spacing and baseline. Retain a clear pictogram and a directional arrow shaped consistently with the doorway's restrained geometry. The whole card remains a native link; there must be no nested button. Focus and preview states remain clear. Photo-source and licence links remain individually usable below the card.

Give photographs enough room. Use one vertical card per row below 480px of actual reading width, balanced horizontal image/text cards from 480px to 959px, and three equal columns only when the reading area reaches 960px. Do not create a two-plus-one grid or shrink French and Arabic titles to squeeze three cards into tablet width. Keep the grid symmetrical and preserve all translated copy.
